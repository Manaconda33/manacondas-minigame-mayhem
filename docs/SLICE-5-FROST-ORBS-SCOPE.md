## Engineering findings

At the authoritative checkpoint, `itemDefinitions.ts` registered three Frost charges without projectile configuration. `ProjectileImpact` and `KartTimeTrial.updateProjectiles()` assumed spinout impacts. The implementation added a typed non-spin effect path rather than passing a zero-duration spin through the existing spin/hit-camera contract. `RacerEffects` received an independently owned timed handling impairment, and `KartController` received a generic neutral-by-default steering modifier rather than an item-name branch in physics. `ItemSystem` cadence ownership was extended without changing accepted Blaze timing or rollback behavior.

The implementation uses an independent Frost stack/timer, typed non-spin impacts, and a neutral-by-default steering modifier. Frost timers advance once at the start of each active simulation step, before controller input and projectile impacts. Expiry removes all stacks before that step's drive; later impacts begin a fresh 1.2-second interval, and each same-step hit immediately updates stacks and target velocity for subsequent Seeker/Blast processing. Paused/countdown steps do not advance Frost.

## Deterministic acceptance fixtures — deployed

- `?testItem=frost-orbs` forces the actual three-charge pickup after collecting a box. Test charge display, cadence, directions and normal flight.
- `?testItem=frost-orbs&testFrost=hit` stages a visibly marked moving rival on a verified clear approach. The real user shot must show measured pre/post planar velocity, Frost duration, and absence of spin. A stationary target cannot prove momentum retention.
- `?testItem=frost-orbs&testFrost=refresh` stages the same measured encounter with enough approach space to land a second shot before expiry. Report first-hit retention, the second cumulative momentum cut, the additional handling stack, and the reset 1.2-second timer. A miss/late hit is inconclusive for refresh, never PASS.
- `?testItem=shockwave&testFrost=shockwave` stages an incoming production Frost orb with a measured 5 m counter window and verifies destruction before contact.
- `?testItem=prismatic-invincibility&testFrost=prismatic&testFrostPhase=protected|expired` uses a controlled moving-player encounter after valid activation or after expiry. The protected case must show absorption without a new slowdown/impairment; the expired control must demonstrate the real effect.

Each scenario requires fixed pickup, held-item readiness, visible setup/stages/results, actual production contact/effect evidence, and clear restart/retry instructions. Test-only setup occurs before measurement, is visibly labeled, and leaves lap/checkpoint authority intact. No AI item acquisition/use; normal URLs remain fixture-free. Counter/impact callbacks alone do not prove an applied velocity/steering effect.

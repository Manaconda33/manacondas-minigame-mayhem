## Object diagnosis and source corrections

The first source audit found that the visible ground was not one standalone floor object. Ground and foundations were merged into structural render meshes in several sectors. The B2 correction therefore edits only the indexed ranges or batched instances that represent decorative ground, while retaining walls, safety geometry, road, shortcut edges, approved signs and the waterfall.

| Reported appearance | Render source and evidence | B2 treatment |

The visible fascia is approximately X −0.1…288 m, Y −7.5…18.7 m, Z −216…45 m. | Suppress 1,186 ground and 792 foundation triangles only.

## Original presentation

A pale-blue faceted orb with a bright white core and a short, finite crystal-particle trail, distinct from Blaze's warm ember effect. Impact produces a small crystal burst. The affected kart carries subtle following frost flecks and a readable FROST countdown during the 1.2-second impairment; no enclosing opaque block, full-screen freeze, spin animation, or persistent ice patch. Fade cleanly at expiry, keeping the driver and course readable in both cameras and on elevated surfaces. Brief original launch/impact tones honor gesture unlock, master volume, pause, unavailable audio and disposal. Use bounded procedural resources; final audio/VFX polish remains Slice 6.

## Engineering findings

At the authoritative checkpoint, `itemDefinitions.ts` registers three Frost charges without projectile configuration. `ProjectileImpact` and `KartTimeTrial.updateProjectiles()` currently assume spinout impacts. They need a typed non-spin effect path; do not pass a zero-duration spin through the existing spin/hit-camera contract. `RacerEffects` has independently owned boosts/protection/spins but no timed handling impairment. `KartController` already exposes planar velocity retention and a steering target; add a generic neutral-by-default steering modifier, not item-name branches in physics. Apply it without scaling raw input or drift charge. `ItemSystem` currently starts cadence specifically for Blaze; extend configuration/ownership carefully while preserving the accepted Blaze timing and rollback contract.

Verify that other live effects coexist without source deletion, repeat impacts in one update share the new Frost state, and snapshots refresh when velocity changes before subsequent Seeker/Blast calculations. Preserve accepted Shockwave-before-projectile ordering and Prismatic protection timing. Frost is approved for implementation but is not implemented or live accepted.

## Deterministic acceptance fixtures — approved, not deployed

- `?testItem=frost-orbs` forces the actual three-charge pickup after collecting a box. Test charge display, cadence, directions and normal flight.
- `?testItem=frost-orbs&testFrost=hit` stages a visibly marked moving rival on a verified clear approach. The real user shot must show measured pre/post planar velocity, Frost duration, and absence of spin. A stationary target cannot prove momentum retention.
- `?testItem=frost-orbs&testFrost=refresh` stages the same measured encounter with enough approach space to land a second shot before expiry. Report first-hit retention, the second cumulative momentum cut, the additional handling stack, and the reset 1.2-second timer. A miss/late hit is inconclusive for refresh, never PASS.
- `?testItem=shockwave&testFrost=shockwave` stages an incoming production Frost orb with a measured 5 m counter window and verifies destruction before contact.
- `?testItem=prismatic-invincibility&testFrost=prismatic&testFrostPhase=protected|expired` uses a controlled moving-player encounter after valid activation or after expiry. The protected case must show absorption without a new slowdown/impairment; the expired control must demonstrate the real effect.

Each scenario requires fixed pickup, held-item readiness, visible setup/stages/results, actual production contact/effect evidence, and clear restart/retry instructions. Test-only setup occurs before measurement, is visibly labeled, and leaves lap/checkpoint authority intact. Define and enforce a suitable road/speed/approach condition during implementation; do not publish links until fixtures reliably achieve their intended outcomes. No AI item acquisition/use; normal URLs remain fixture-free. Counter/impact callbacks alone do not prove an applied velocity/steering effect.
